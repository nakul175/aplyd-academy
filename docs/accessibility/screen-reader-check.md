# Manual screen-reader check on a phone (ACA-US-12.3, AC2): protocol and record

**Status: not yet done.** Needs a tester with an Android phone (TalkBack) or an iPhone (VoiceOver), on the local or staging setup with synthetic accounts only.

## Steps (main journey: join, task, practice, submit)

1. Open an invite link. Confirm the notice is read in order and the age question buttons are announced.
2. Sign in with the emailed code. Confirm the code field is labelled and errors are announced.
3. Tick the required consent and one reminder; join. Confirm checkboxes announce their label and state.
4. Take the placement check with the screen reader only. Confirm each question is a group with its options.
5. Open the practice task. Move by headings: task, checked guidance, coach, attempt, finish.
6. Attach a file from the phone; confirm the progress bar and the "Uploaded" message are announced.
7. Ask the coach; confirm the reply is announced.
8. Save an attempt; submit with a note. Confirm the success message is announced.
9. Open Help from the task; report a problem.
