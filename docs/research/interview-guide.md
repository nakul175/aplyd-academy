# Day-90 learner interviews (ACA-US-10.3): guide, consent record and summary template

**Who:** consenting learners among the most and least active (Operations → cohort → Evidence lists them; only people who turned on "I may be invited to an interview" appear).

## Consent at the start (read aloud, then record in the app)
"Thank you for talking to us. This takes about 20 minutes. We will take notes, not a recording. Your name will not appear in anything we share; we use what you say to improve the programme. You can stop at any time or ask us to delete your notes. Is that all right?" Tick "The learner confirmed consent" only if they say yes.

## Questions
1. Think back to the workshop. What did you expect the weekly practice to be like?
2. Tell me about a week when you did the task. What made it possible?
3. Tell me about a week when you did not. What got in the way?
4. Which task was most useful for your real work? What changed because of it?
5. How did you use the AI coach's hints? Was there a time it was wrong or unhelpful?
6. Did the reminders help, annoy, or make no difference? Which channel?
7. If you could change one thing about the weekly practice, what would it be?

## Anonymised summary (what goes into the day-90 review)
- Activity group: most active / least active
- What helped them practise (2–3 points, no names, no employer, no identifying details)
- What got in the way (2–3 points)
- One change they would make
- Any harm or worry raised (tell the owner the same day)

The app refuses a summary that contains the learner's name or email.
