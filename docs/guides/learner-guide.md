# Learner guide: practising with Aplyd Academy

1. **Join.** Open the invite link from your association. Read how your data is used, say you are 18 or older, sign in with the code we email you, and choose your reminders.
2. **Placement check.** Five quick questions set your AI level. It is not a test. If the level feels wrong, ask for a review on the same screen.
3. **At the workshop (week 0).** Your facilitator starts the practice session. Open your first task, attach something from your own work, ask the coach for a hint, and submit a short note.
4. **Every week.** A new task appears on your home screen, and a reminder arrives if you chose one. Bring your own work, try it, use the coach for hints, save your attempt to see feedback and a worked answer, then submit what changed.
5. **Checks and catch-the-AI.** Sometimes you get a short check with AI help switched off, and once a month a task where you spot planted AI mistakes. These show real skill; they are not grades.
6. **Live sessions.** You may be invited to an online session with people at your level, followed by a thread for questions.
7. **Your data.** Only you see your files and notes. Your association sees group numbers only. In Settings you can change reminders, download your data, or delete your account.
8. **Help.** Tap Help on any screen to read answers or report a problem.
