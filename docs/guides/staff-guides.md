# Staff guides

## Association admin
- **Set up:** open the invite from APLYD, join, open "Organisation and cohorts", create the cohort with the workshop date (access lasts one year from it).
- **Enrol:** paste one member per line: `name, email, role, mobile (optional)`. Rows with problems are shown with the reason and are not invited; correct and paste them again. Watch "Invite status"; resend a link if needed.
- **Invite** facilitators and the sector expert from the organisation page.
- **Progress:** the cohort page shows group numbers only. Small groups are hidden. You never see anyone's files, notes or answers.

## Facilitator
- **Workshop:** "Start the workshop practice session" gives every placed participant a first task; the session view shows who has finished, never their work.
- **Follow up:** the list shows people who stopped practising for the number of weeks the owner set; log what happened. They leave the list after they practise again.
- **Level reviews:** decide each request; the change is logged.
- **Checks:** give a published unaided check to the cohort.
- **Sessions and community:** schedule a session for one level, end it to open the thread, hide reported posts.
- **Help requests** arrive on the facilitate page. Security or privacy reports go straight to the owner.

## Sector expert or reviewer
- Open your pack from Home. Approve, correct or reject each item; only approved items are published, and only when you publish a new version. Earlier versions are kept.
- Resolve flags in the error log. Before version 2, every logged error needs a proposed fix or a reason for no change ("Next version from the error log").

## APLYD operator and the owner
- Operations: create organisations and invite their admins; create packs, name reviewers, import or draft items; attach packs to cohorts; run the AI evaluation; log incidents; prepare reports.
- Only the owner records decisions (the table shows what is waiting), approves reports, records A-01 and review decisions, switches AI off, reads security reports and accepts or rejects remaining risks.
