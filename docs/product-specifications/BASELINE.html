Aplyd Academy shared design baseline

Version 0.1 dated 5 October 2026. Proposed design derived from the BRD in this conversation. No stakeholder signoff, production implementation, test execution, external accreditation, or agreed service commitments are implied.

Scope and release boundary

R1 pilot: adults; responsive web; selected languages to be decided; direct registration and one partner identity integration; profile with multiple role tags; catalog and immutable program versions; tenant offerings; self paced and facilitated cohorts; resumable learning; practical assessments; human assessment and appeal; completion and competency credentials; moderated cohort community; support; consent and scoped reporting; one partner deep link, enrollment, authorized achievement exchange. AI instruction uses approved external tools and synthetic exercises. No embedded AI assistant in R1.

R2: live session scheduling, diagnostics, explainable recommendations, selected offline packages, professional networking, mentor workflow, refreshers and renewal, commercial entitlements before any paid launch, more languages and partners.

R3: richer personalization and portfolio, additional integrations, optional AI assistant after explicit design review. Credential correction and revocation, security, access controls, accessibility, audit, and privacy apply in R1. Offline submissions and paid commerce are not R1 features.

Architecture and tenancy

Proposed cloud neutral modular monolith with responsive web, backend API, relational transaction store, private object storage, asynchronous worker and transactional outbox; managed identity adapter. No provider or programming framework selected. PostgreSQL compatible DDL and TypeScript shaped contracts may be supplied as reference implementation drafts, not approved stack choices.

Account is global identity. Learner private profile and consent are platform scoped. A tenant is a delivery workspace for a partner or direct Academy. Ecosystem is a contextual configuration such as Agri Saha or MSME; it is not itself the security boundary. Organization is a tenant scoped sponsoring body. Membership grants role plus tenant and optionally organization/cohort scope. Switching learner role tags does not grant permissions. Global published catalog items may be offered by tenants; draft content is author scoped. Offerings, enrollments, cohorts, submissions, decisions, credentials, forums, and reports are tenant scoped. Learner may view a personal aggregate of their own permitted learning records. Partner never gets other tenants' records through that aggregate. Partner identity linkage uses issuer plus subject, never email alone.

Canonical identifiers and rules

Requirement IDs BR-01 to BR-46 exactly match prior BRD. Functional modules F-01 Identity and context; F-02 Discovery and enrollment; F-03 Learning and cohorts; F-04 Responsible AI practice; F-05 Assessments and appeal; F-06 Credentials; F-07 Community and networking; F-08 Content and administration; F-09 Ecosystem integration; F-10 Privacy support and reporting; F-11 Continuous learning; F-12 Commerce. Screen IDs WF-01 Onboarding; WF-02 Home; WF-03 Catalog; WF-04 Pathway details; WF-05 Learning; WF-06 Assessment; WF-07 Feedback and appeal; WF-08 Credential; WF-09 Community; WF-10 Assessor queue; WF-11 Admin; WF-12 Integration and sharing; WF-13 Mentor directory; WF-14 Continuous learning.

Enrollment stores immutable program_version_id and policy snapshot. An enrollment is invited, active, completed, withdrawn or suspended; invitations accept into active. No completion from client percentages. Completed status requires mandatory activities plus final passing assessment when the pathway requires assessment. Activity progress states not_started, in_progress, completed; approved lesson completion rule is server checked. Attempts are draft, submitted, under_review, passed, failed or voided. Retrying creates a new attempt within published policy; an appeal does not mutate a historical decision. Appeal states opened, under_review, upheld, overturned, closed; upheld means original decision stands, overturned results in a new superseding decision.

Assessment rubric has task_quality, verification, information_handling, judgment dimensions. Reference policy: minimum 70 of 100 overall and all critical criteria individually met; critical failure cannot be averaged away. Programs configure and publish their own thresholds before enrollment. Credential kinds participation, completion, competency. Credential states issued, suspended, revoked, expired. Issue is idempotent by enrollment plus credential kind plus standard version. Public verification uses an unguessable verification token and explicit learner sharing choice; default private. Revocation is auditable; historical awards retained as restricted records according to retention policy. Public response has issuer, learner approved display label, achievement and status only; no submissions, scores or other tenant affiliations. Expiry if used is evaluated against current server time as well as stored state.

API and event conventions

/api/v1; JSON; UUID identifiers; timestamps UTC RFC3339; display timezone profile local; cursor pagination; correlation ID; ETag and If-Match for mutable drafts and conflicting updates. Error object code, message, correlation_id, field_errors; codes 400 invalid input, 401 unauthenticated, 403 forbidden where object presence may be disclosed, 404 absent or out of scope, 409 conflict or key reuse with changed body, 412 stale ETag, 422 business rule failure, 429 throttled, 503 transient unavailability. Mutating retryable endpoints require Idempotency-Key scoped to actor plus tenant plus route; same body returns original response; changed body 409. Do not place plaintext PII in logs or URLs.

Partner endpoints /api/v1/partner/enrollments, /api/v1/partner/achievements and /api/v1/partner/webhook-subscriptions. OAuth client scope plus tenant and purpose authorization; OIDC browser login via approved identity adapter; test identity collisions and disabled memberships. Transactions write domain state plus outbox atomically. Events include event_id, event_type, version, occurred_at, tenant_id, subject reference, minimal permitted payload. At least once delivery; consumer dedupes event_id; HMAC signature over timestamp plus raw body; replay window 5 minutes proposed; retries with backoff and jitter, dead letter after bounded attempts, controlled replay. Consent checked at send and replay, not only event creation. Webhook endpoint validation must block private/internal destinations and unsafe redirects. No arbitrary external calls from prototype.

Targets and unanswered decisions

Proposed pilot load: 1000 monthly active learners, 100 concurrent sessions, 10 requests per second steady and 30 per second for 5 minute burst. Provisional p95 API interactive reads 800ms excluding media and partner latency, first meaningful learner content <=3s on defined simulated network, 99.5 percent monthly availability excluding agreed maintenance, RPO 24h and RTO 8h for pilot. These are engineering test hypotheses, not SLA. Data residency, retention durations, first partner, languages, identity provider, hosting, technology stack, credential issuer and recognition, support hours, budgets and cohort rules remain open. Cases use synthetic data. Test suite execution status Not run throughout.

Reference model refinements

The detailed R1 schema adds program_competency, criterion_score, webhook_subscription and agreement_acceptance as relational bridges or explicit acceptance records. Required service/community agreements are distinct from optional sharing grants. Public verification and partner achievement sharing are independent. Public verification exposes only issuer label, approved learner display label, achievement title and current effective status. Partner achievement queries use an opaque learner_ref resolved under current tenant, client, purpose and resource permissions; external identity values are not placed in query URLs.
