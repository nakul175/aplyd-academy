**How Aplyd Academy uses your data**

Aplyd Academy is run by APLYD [TO CONFIRM: legal entity name and address]. Your association invited you to practise using AI on your own work for one year.

**What we collect**
- Your name, email and job role, from your association's list.
- Your mobile number, only if you choose WhatsApp or SMS reminders.
- Your answers to the short placement check and your AI level.
- What you do in the app: tasks, your attempts, your questions to the AI coach and its replies, your notes, check results, and files you choose to upload.

**Why**
- To give you weekly practice tasks at your level and remind you, if you choose.
- To show you your own history.
- To count, for your whole group, how many people practise each week. Your association sees only these group numbers, never your files, notes or answers.

**AI**
- Your questions to the coach are sent to an AI provider to get a reply [TO CONFIRM: provider and where it processes data, after the legal review].
- Your files are not sent to the AI provider unless this notice is updated to say so.
- Your data is never used to train AI models.

**How long**
- We keep your data while you have access and then as the law requires or allows [TO CONFIRM: periods per record type, recorded as `retention.periods`].
- You can ask us to delete your data at any time: go to Settings, then Delete my data. It is deleted, not just hidden.

**Your choices and rights**
- Reminders, quoting your notes without your name, and interviews are optional. You can turn each one on or off in Settings at any time.
- You can download your data at any time from Settings.
- You must be 18 or older to join.
- Questions or complaints: [TO CONFIRM: grievance contact under the DPDP Rules]. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
