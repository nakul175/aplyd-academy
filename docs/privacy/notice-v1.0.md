**How Aplyd Academy uses your data**

Aplyd Academy is run by APLYD (an Athena Infonomics organisation). Your association invited you to practise using AI on your own work for one year. Questions about your data: nakul.jain@aplyd.com.

**What we collect**

- Your name, email and job role, from your association's list.
- Your mobile number, only if you choose WhatsApp or SMS reminders.
- Your answers to the short placement check and your AI level.
- What you do in the app: tasks, your attempts, your questions to the AI coach and its replies, your notes, check results, and files you choose to upload.

**Why**

- To give you weekly practice tasks at your level and remind you, if you choose.
- To show you your own history.
- To count, for your whole group, how many people practise each week. Your association sees only these group numbers, never your files, notes or answers. Numbers for groups smaller than 5 people are not shown.

**AI**

- Your questions to the coach, and the task you are working on, are sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to get a reply. Anthropic processes this data outside India, does not use it to train its models, and deletes it within 30 days (content its safety systems flag as misuse may be kept for up to 2 years).
- Your uploaded files are never sent to the AI provider.
- Your data is never used to train AI models, by us or by anyone else.

**How long**

- We keep your practice history, notes and files while you have access and afterwards, until you ask us to delete them. Sign-in codes are deleted after 1 day, sign-in sessions after 30 days, unused invitations after 30 days, and reminder messages after 90 days.
- You can ask us to delete your data at any time: go to Settings, then Delete my data. It is deleted, not just hidden, and the deletion is repeated on any backup we restore.

**Your choices and rights**

- Reminders, quoting your notes without your name, and interviews are optional. You can turn each one on or off in Settings at any time.
- You can download your data at any time from Settings.
- You must be 18 or older to join. Aplyd Academy is for adults at work; we do not knowingly hold data about anyone under 18.
- Questions or complaints: write to nakul.jain@aplyd.com with the subject "Aplyd Academy data". We reply within 7 working days. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
