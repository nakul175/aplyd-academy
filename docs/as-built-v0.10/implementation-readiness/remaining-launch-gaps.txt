Required before a real supervised pilot

1. Hosted recipient and trusted identity (PR-18): use authorized actual accounts
   to prove private hosting permission and Academy invitation separately, account
   identity continuity and refusal of impersonating client headers. Local synthetic
   identity fixtures establish application behavior only. Keep owner-private
   hosting until the intended recipients' access is explicitly arranged.
2. Representative UAT (PR-19): farmers, MSME owners and public-service users must
   complete chosen real tasks with permitted information, check/correct an artifact
   and explain remaining uncertainty. Record assistance, language, literacy,
   connectivity and device barriers. Three English draft pathways and governance
   reference archives are not all-audience validation or measured productivity.
3. Named staff and observed operations (PR-20): recruit and calibrate assessors and
   independent reviewers; assign support/moderation/escalation responsibilities;
   test actual handoffs and coverage. An in-app queue does not establish response
   staffing, a deadline, an emergency service or accommodation approval.
4. Content and institutional approval: review sector examples, translations,
   verification/AI-use procedures and information classification with accountable
   owners. Supplied source statistics/legal maps and composite cases remain
   teaching references. Frontline override authority, protection from retaliation
   and escalation arrangements are explicitly unresolved locally. Agree them
   before exercises are used as workplace instructions.
5. Retention, privacy and recovery operations: agree actual retention/erasure,
   access reviews, incident handling and backup ownership; demonstrate restore
   and recovery against an approved dataset. Injected local transaction rollback
   is not backup restoration or production-outage recovery.

Beyond the current pilot, before corresponding public claims

A live Agri Saha/MSME/public-service partner needs an authenticated contract,
field mapping, delivery/receipt, revocation-at-recipient, failure and interoperability
verification. The current workbench creates local historical receipts only; no
partner is contacted. Health remains blocked pending domain review.

Recognized certification needs an approved issuer, qualification rules, issuance/
revocation governance and public verification arrangements. Existing practice
records are nonaccredited; downloaded source certificate templates confer none.

Embedded AI, approved employer tools, delivered reminders, meeting-provider
booking, recordings, direct messages, uploads, commerce and offline assisted
journeys are not implied by present screens. Evaluate them as separate features;
no automatic sender or service-level promise has been introduced.
