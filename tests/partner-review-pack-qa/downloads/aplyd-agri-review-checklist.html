Current local contract and field definitions

Version aplyd.learning-return/1.0-test · Purpose local-return-contract-review · Mode local-test · Partner agri

A local choice selects one or both summary scopes for exactly 1, 7 or 30 days. It is tied to one saved enrollment and its original global content version. At most 24 choices and 100 local receipts are retained. These limits describe the simulator, not a partner service.

Boundary checks to discuss

These checks describe the current local model. A live partner design still needs its own acceptance evidence and responsible reviewers.

  1. Add a name, score, note or any unsupported payload field.
    Reject the return. No extra field is transmitted or accepted by this local contract.
  2. Omit a selected summary, add an unselected summary, repeat a scope or use an unsupported scope.
    Reject the return; selected fields and summary objects must agree.
  3. Use another mapped pathway, private workspace pin or a different retained version.
    Reject a new local test. Do not substitute the current curriculum for an unavailable pin.
  4. Run a new test at the exact saved expiry time.
    Reject the new test at now >= expiry. Earlier valid receipts remain historical local snapshots.
  5. Withdraw a valid retained choice, then try a new test.
    Reject the new test. Preserve earlier receipts; withdrawal is not a partner deletion promise.
  6. Withdraw a valid retained choice while learning is paused or retained receipt history is malformed.
    Allow the independent withdrawal. Do not require a new test or readable receipt history to stop future local tests.
  7. Present a receipt created in the future, before its retained choice, at/after expiry or after withdrawal.
    Reject that receipt history. Current time and retained choice timing are checked; a valid old receipt is not current permission.
  8. Reach 24 retained choices or 100 retained receipts.
    Reject a further choice or test respectively. Keep retained history and independent withdrawal available.

The server checks the current saved choice and pinned learning for every new local test. Existing valid receipts remain snapshots after expiry, withdrawal or a later practice-status change.
